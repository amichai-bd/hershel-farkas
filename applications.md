## Mathematics: solving the Schottky problem with physics equations
- T. Shiota, "Characterization of Jacobian varieties in terms of soliton equations," *Invent. Math.* 83 (1986). [doi:10.1007/BF01388967](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01388967) — a period matrix is a Jacobian's iff its theta function solves the KP equation (Novikov's conjecture).
- I. Krichever, "Characterizing Jacobians via trisecants of the Kummer variety," *Ann. of Math.* 172 (2010). [doi:10.4007/annals.2010.172.485](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2010.172.485) — trisecant characterization, related to Fay's trisecant formula (a topic of Farkas's 1984 and 1992 papers).

## Physics: string theory
- E. D'Hoker, D. H. Phong, "Two-Loop Superstrings I, Main Formulas," [arXiv:hep-th/0110247](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110247) — string amplitudes are integrals over moduli of Riemann surfaces, expressed via theta constants.

## Computer science: cryptography
- N. Koblitz, "Hyperelliptic cryptosystems," *J. Cryptology* 1 (1989). [doi:10.1007/BF02252872](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02252872) — public-key cryptography in the Jacobians of hyperelliptic curves.

- Algebraic-geometry (Goppa) codes are built from algebraic curves over finite fields (the algebraic counterpart of compact Riemann surfaces). M. A. Tsfasman, S. G. Vlăduţ, T.
